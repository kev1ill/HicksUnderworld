BITCADE FREE SFX PACK - VOL. 1

30 retro game sound effects in 8-bit and 16-bit style, designed in Bitcade.

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
WAV/              44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, normalized to -1 dBFS
    Coin        3 sounds
    Explosion   3 sounds
    Hit         4 sounds
    Jingle      3 sounds
    Jump        4 sounds
    Laser       3 sounds
    Magic       3 sounds
    Power-Up    3 sounds
    UI          4 sounds
Bitcade-Presets/  the Bitcade presets behind every sound. Many sounds layer two or
                  three presets or play a short note sequence; the layers are numbered
                  (for example "SFX Explosion Big 1"). Own Bitcade? Copy them into
                  Documents\Bitcade\Presets, open them in Bitcade and tweak away.

WANT MORE?
----------
Bitcade makes sounds like these in one click: pick a category (Coin, Jump, Laser,
Explosion, Power-Up, Hit, Blip ...), click RANDOM, then drag the WAV into your game.
It is also a full retro synth for music with four engines: chip, 8-bit computer,
16-bit FM and sampler (VST3 plugin and standalone app, Windows).

Get Bitcade: https://schniersky.itch.io/bitcade
